Privacy Policy

Last updated: May 15th 2025

Job Alert Filter is a Google Sheets add-on that reads job-alert emails from Gmail and writes extracted job listings into your own Google Sheet. This page explains what data the add-on accesses and how it is handled.

What data is accessed

  • Gmail messages — the add-on searches your Gmail inbox for emails from two specific job-alert senders: jobalerts-noreply@linkedin.com (LinkedIn) and donotreply@jobalert.indeed.com (Indeed). Only the HTML body of those emails is read, solely to extract job title, company name, location, and a link to the job posting. No other emails are accessed.
  • Google Sheet data — the add-on reads and writes only the active spreadsheet to which it is installed. It creates two tabs ("LinkedIn Jobs" and "Indeed Jobs") and writes the extracted job rows there.
  • Script Properties — keyword lists and preferences you configure in the settings sidebar are stored in Apps Script Script Properties, which are scoped to your script and accessible only to you.

What data is NOT collected or shared

  • No data is transmitted to any external server or third party.
  • No data is stored outside your own Google account.
  • The add-on does not read, store, or process any email other than the two job-alert senders listed above.
  • The add-on does not collect any personally identifiable information.

Google API scopes used

  • gmail.readonly — to search for and read job-alert emails from the two senders above.
  • spreadsheets.currentonly — to write job listings into the active Google Sheet.
  • script.scriptapp — to create the optional daily scan trigger.
  • script.storage — to save your keyword preferences in Script Properties.
  • script.container.ui — to display the menu and settings sidebar inside Google Sheets.

Data retention

All data (job rows in the Sheet, keyword preferences in Script Properties) remains entirely within your Google account. You can delete it at any time by clearing the sheet tabs or removing the Script Properties via the control panel UI.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes materially, the "Last updated" date above will be revised. Continued use of the add-on after changes constitutes acceptance of the updated policy.
