Job Alert Filter

A Google Sheets add-on that scans LinkedIn and Indeed job-alert emails and automatically organizes matching jobs into your Google Sheet.

Features

  • Automatic email scanning Reads daily job-alert emails from LinkedIn and Indeed directly from your Gmail inbox.
  • Keyword scoring Scores every listing by job title, industry domain, and location keywords — each match adds a point.
  • Work-type bonus Remote roles get +2 pts, hybrid +1, so the jobs you actually want float to the top.
  • Deduplication The same job is never added twice, even if it appears in multiple alert emails.
  • Application status tracking Dropdown column (Not Applied → Applied → Pass) with color-coding lets you track every listing at a glance.
  • Daily auto-scan A time-based Apps Script trigger runs the scan every morning at 07:00 — no manual action needed.
  • Bilingual UI Switch between English and Chinese(中文) from the menu at any time.
  • Private by design All processing happens inside Google's infrastructure. No data ever leaves your Google account.

How it works

1.Gmail receives your daily LinkedIn / Indeed job-alert emails
2.The add-on parses each email to extract job title, company, location, and work type
3.Each listing is scored against your keyword preferences
4.New jobs are written into your Google Sheet (LinkedIn and Indeed on separate tabs)
5.Rows are sorted by application status, then by score — highest match first
