Job Alert Filter

A Google Sheets add-on that scans LinkedIn and Indeed job-alert emails and automatically organizes matching jobs into your Google Sheet.

Features

  • Automatic email scanning Reads daily job-alert emails from LinkedIn and Indeed directly from your Gmail inbox.
  • Keyword scoring Scores every listing by job title, industry domain, and location keywords — each match adds a point.
  • Work-type bonus Remote roles get +2 pts, hybrid +1, so the jobs you actually want float to the top.
  • Deduplication The same job is never added twice, even if it appears in multiple alert emails.
  • Application status tracking Dropdown column (Not Applied → Applied → Pass) with color-coding lets you track every listing at a glance.
  • Daily auto-scan A time-based Apps Script trigger runs the scan every morning at 07:00 — no manual action needed.
  • Bilingual UI Switch between English and Chinese(中文) from the menu at any time.
  • Private by design All processing happens inside Google's infrastructure. No data ever leaves your Google account.

How it works

1.Gmail receives your daily LinkedIn / Indeed job-alert emails
2.The add-on parses each email to extract job title, company, location, and work type
3.Each listing is scored against your keyword preferences
4.New jobs are written into your Google Sheet (LinkedIn and Indeed on separate tabs)
5.Rows are sorted by application status, then by score — highest match first

Data Access & Permissions

Job Alert Filter requests the minimum Google account permissions needed to do its job. Below is every permission the app requests and the exact reason it needs it.

PermissionWhy the app needs it
Read Gmail messages The app searches your inbox for job-alert emails sent by LinkedIn and Indeed. It reads only the emails that match those senders — it never accesses, stores, or transmits any other email.
Edit the current Google Sheet Parsed job listings are written into the spreadsheet where the add-on is installed. The app cannot access any other spreadsheet in your Drive.
Manage time-based triggers The optional daily auto-scan installs a single Apps Script trigger that runs the scan every morning at 07:00. You can remove this trigger at any time from the Apps Script dashboard.
Store script properties Your keyword preferences and language setting are saved in Apps Script's ScriptProperties store — a key-value store private to this script. No data is sent to any external server.
Show menus and sidebars The settings sidebar and the Job Alert Filter menu in Google Sheets require UI permission to render inside the Sheets interface.

All processing runs entirely inside Google's infrastructure (Google Apps Script). No job data, email content, or personal information is ever sent to or stored on any external server.
